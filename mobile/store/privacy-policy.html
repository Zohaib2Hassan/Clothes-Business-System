Threadly Pocket privacy policy

Prepared 23 October 2026 for version 1.1.0.

Website account and shared shop records

When you connect a website, account registration/sign-in and shop requests are sent over HTTPS to that address. Your website stores account/contact information, product details and selected product photos, stock, customers, sales, payment/refund records, expenses, notifications and business settings in its database. These records are shared with devices signed in to the same account. Website hosting and service providers process this information to operate the service. Use a website address you trust. The temporary testing tunnel is provided through Cloudflare and depends on the developer's computer remaining online.

Sign-in storage

The app does not save your account password. A session token is stored using platform SecureStore on Android and iPhone. The browser preview uses browser session storage. The selected website address is saved in app storage. Sign out to remove the stored session. Device security and operating-system backup settings also apply.

Photos, AI and payments

A product photo is uploaded only when you select and save it. No general photo-library access or camera access is required. If you use AI chat, your question, recent conversation and relevant shop context are processed by the website's configured AI provider. Forecasts depend on the website's model/service. Review the website's own provider policies. The app does not collect card numbers or execute payments/refunds; it records transactions handled outside the app. This version includes no advertising or developer analytics integration.

Separate on-device shop

The optional earlier on-device mode stores records with AsyncStorage and can work offline. Its records are not automatically uploaded, merged or synchronized with your website account. The app does not add encryption to this local shop database. The optional sample shop contains labelled fictional records.

Export and sharing

When you choose an export or receipt share, the app creates CSV, JSON backup (on-device mode) or receipt files and opens a file/share interface. You select the destination. Files may contain customer and business details. Keep them private and manage copies at the destination. Temporary files can remain in app cache until cleared.

Deleting data

In connected mode, Account & security offers permanent deletion of the website account and its shop records. Sign-out removes the app session but does not delete server records. In on-device mode, More offers erase/start-fresh. Uninstalling or clearing storage may remove local records; platform secure storage and OS backups can follow platform retention behavior. These actions do not remove exported files, recipient copies or OS backups. Manage those separately.
