# Threadly Pocket ? store copy draft

Short description: Manage your clothing shop's stock, sales and customers from your phone.

Threadly Pocket brings your Threadly website account to a fresh mobile design. Sign in to see shared stock, customers, sales, expenses and shop settings. Add product photos and variants, adjust inventory, hold a bag, record a received payment, export receipts, process recorded returns and review reports. Ask the website assistant or view forecasts when those services are configured.

Changes are saved to the website and appear on refresh across connected devices. Online features require internet and a running compatible Threadly website. Payments and refunds are records only; the app does not move money. Website subscription status is available, with no native subscription purchase in this release.

An optional separate on-device shop retains offline records, labelled sample data and JSON backup/restore. It does not automatically merge with website records.
